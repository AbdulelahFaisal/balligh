## Source library content

The library under `content/library/` is third-party religious content, stored unchanged for reading. It is not covered by the project's MIT licence (`LICENSE` covers project-owned code only); each publisher's own terms apply. Full notices, quoted terms, versions and retrieval times are in `content/notices/SOURCE_NOTICES_G4-A_ingestion.md` (a byte-identical copy of `docs/review/G4-A/SOURCE_NOTICES.md`), with the later corrections and use limits in `content/notices/SOURCE_NOTICES_G4-A-P1_corrections.md`. Those notices are kept unchanged as archived evidence, so their phrase "none chosen yet" predates `LICENSE`; the MIT licence still does not extend to the library.

| Asset | Origin | Rights status |
|---|---|---|
| `content/library/quran/` | QuranEnc.com (Encyclopedia of the Noble Quran) API: Arabic text and six translations of the meanings (`english_rwwad`, `urdu_junagarhi`, `chinese_suliman`, `indonesian_affairs`, `bengali_rwwad`, `french_rashid`) and the Arabic Al-Tafsir Al-Muyassar (`arabic_moyassar`; original publisher King Fahd Quran Printing Complex, delivered via QuranEnc) | QuranEnc's published re-publication terms: no modification; cite the publisher and QuranEnc.com; mention the version; keep transcript information; report notes to QuranEnc; update to the latest version; no inappropriate advertisements. Versions are pinned in the index. |
| `content/library/fatwa/` | binbaz.org.sa, the official site of Sheikh Abdulaziz Ibn Baz | The user reports email permission from the site for this project (user-confirmed; the email was not inspected by the implementer; not a public-domain status). Attribution and a link to each fatwa page are kept. The site's honorific font is not bundled; permission to redistribute it has not been established. |
| `content/library/hadith/` | HadeethEnc.com (Encyclopedia of Translated Prophetic Hadiths) API, Arabic and six translations | HadeethEnc's own terms apply (see `SOURCE_NOTICES.md`); the Ibn Baz/Dorar permission does not replace them. ## Caption test fixtures

`server/tests/fixtures/json3/` holds deterministic neutral Arabic captions written by the Balligh team for parser tests (project-owned, MIT). The earlier caption samples of unknown rights are not part of this repository.

## AI-assisted translations

`content/translations/fatwa/` holds translations generated with DeepSeek (`deepseek-v4-pro`) from the Ibn Baz fatwa texts. They are labelled AI-assisted in the app, are not publisher translations, and the underlying fatwa texts keep their publisher's terms.
